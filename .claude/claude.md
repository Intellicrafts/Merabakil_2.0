# Project Instructions

## Product Context

This is a business-oriented legal platform connecting clients with
relevant lawyers based on their legal case.

The platform includes:

- AI-powered case discovery
- Text and voice-based case conversations
- Lawyer recommendations
- Curated case opportunities for lawyers
- Case management
- Client and lawyer document registry
- Legal document drafting
- AI-assisted workflows

The primary personas are:

- Clients
- Lawyers / Advocates

The product should feel professional, trustworthy, secure,
business-oriented, and user-centric.

## UI/UX

For any UI/UX, frontend, design, layout, interaction, dashboard,
workflow, or user-experience task:

- Follow `.claude/skills/ui-ux-engineering/SKILL.md`.
- Prioritize user goals and business workflows over visual novelty.
- Reuse existing components and design patterns.
- Do not introduce arbitrary UI patterns, colors, spacing, or components.
- Consider loading, empty, error, success, disabled, responsive,
  and accessibility states.
- Perform visual/UX review before considering major UI work complete.

## General Engineering Principles

- Inspect the existing code before making architectural changes.
- Reuse existing components and utilities.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Keep changes focused on the requested scope.
- Do not break existing workflows.
- Follow the project's existing architecture and conventions.
