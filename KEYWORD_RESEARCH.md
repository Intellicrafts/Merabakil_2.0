## 💰 ROI Calculation

### Assumptions
- Avg. visitor value: ₹5,000 (marketplace booking value)
- Conversion rate: 2-5%
- Avg. CPC (if paid): ₹50-100

### Organic vs. Paid Comparison

**Organic (6-month timeline):**
- Cost: 1 person × 6 months (content + SEO) = ~₹3-5 lakhs
- Revenue generated: 3000 visits × 3% × ₹5000 = ₹45 lakhs/year
- ROI: +9x

**Paid Ads (same budget):**
- Cost: ₹3-5 lakhs campaign budget
- Revenue generated: 60,000 clicks × ₹100 CPC ÷ 3% × ₹5000 = ₹100 lakhs/year
- ROI: +20x (but unsustainable long-term)

**Hybrid (Recommended):**
- Organic provides stable, recurring traffic
- Paid ads accelerate growth in months 1-3
- Combined ROI: +15x over 12 months
